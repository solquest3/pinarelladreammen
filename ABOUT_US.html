ABOUT THE PINARELLAS
The Pinarella Dream Men get the origin in Faenza from a weird idea in July 2004. We got engaged for a hen night without any experience at all, but with a full immersion of music and dancing with the manager and the choreographer we got ready for the debut.
The members of the original group were Mr Pink, Mr Green e Mr Blue (the broker), even if the flash underpants were not even thought for that strip.
The show (?) was done in a beach in Pinarella di Cervia, that's where we got our name.
After the first one there's a sabbatical year -not due to us but to the lack of engagements- and in May 2006 we got contacted for another hen night, in a beach again -but this time under the rain.
For that occasion Mr Orange joined the group and we had the idea of the coloured underpants.
YOUNG PINARELLAS GROW UP
If 2004 was the year of the debut, 2005 the one of the rest and 2006 the one of the rebirth, 2007 is definitely the year of the consecration.
We got engaged for six hen nights and one graduation party between May and December, and the name of Pinarellas arrives also in other districts.
This is also the year when Mr Blue put the underpants away, leaving the Dream Men.
Since the strip ot the 3rd of June his place was taken by Mr Yellow, so the members of the Pinarellas are four again.
2008 starts following in 2007's footsteps, seen that Pinarellas got recruited by a local disco pub for Women's Day. And thanks to the idea, thought with the manager and the coreographer, of getting same colour bib aprons, it's a triumph!
That day is followed by a hen night, but then, due to Mr Pink's Russian campaingn, there are only refusals. At the end of the year, there will be three refused engagements, all of the for the lack of minimum number of Pinarellas.
