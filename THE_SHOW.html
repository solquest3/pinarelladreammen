PINARELLAS' SHOW
the rose
the wine
the handcuffs
the cherries
The original show was quite simple including only one song, You sexy thing by the Hot Chocolate, during which the Pinarellas, starting from the classic Reservoir Dogs' suit, ended up in their traditional clothes.
In June 2007 the choreographer is persuaded that the Pinarellas must be raised and completely revolutionizes the show.
So they get to the new show, much more complex than the former one.
Now the strip is formed by three parts:
1) an initial arabic stage, during the song Wahrane by Khaled, where the Pinarellas appear wrapped up in cloaks and turbans. The first two carry on a kind of romantic courting, while the last two of pure temptation.
It starts with Mr Orange offering the bride a bottle of sweet wine, with which all the Pinarellas drink the girl's happiness.
He's followed by Mr Pink who gives her a rose held among his teeth, as an Argentine tanguero.
Ended this stage of courting, it starts the one in which the last two Pinarellas embody the temptation.
Mr Green appeals to lust, teasing the girl and handcuffing her.
The last is Mr Yellow who recalls the sin of gluttony, tempting the bride with red passion cherries.
After all these offers, the Pinarellas take off the coaks and turbants, revealing their faces.
2) The second stage is the one of the proper classic strip, on the notes of Knock on wood by David Bowie. During this song the Pinarellas free themselves from the cumbersome Reservoir Dogs' suits and on the last note reveal their famous coloured underpants.
3) The last part of the show takes place while the Right Said Fred sing I'm too sexy for my shirt.
Now the Pinarellas show off their bodies, and after having invited the bride and her friends to dance, they take the lucky one on their shoulders, and disappear throught the horizon.
Since 2008's shows there's one more change of clothes: now the Pinarellas end the night wearing only their wonderful same-colour bib aprons.
